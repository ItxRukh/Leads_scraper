## What we build
### Client Access
- **Missed-Call Text-Back** — Texts back clients who could not get through, asks what they need in administrative terms, and puts the request in the queue. Result: Clients who hit a busy line get a reply instead of calling the emergency hospital. Needs from you: business calling and approved messaging flow. Typical time: 3–5 business days.
- **Appointment Request Capture** — Collects patient, reason-for-visit and preferred timing on your approved fields, then books or hands a clean request to reception. Result: Requests arrive complete, so reception is not calling back for the pet’s name and age. Needs from you: approved intake fields and booking rules. Typical time: 5–8 business days.
- **Approved Client FAQ Assistant** — Answers only the questions you approve — hours, location, parking, payment options, what to bring — and hands over anything else. Result: Routine questions stop pulling staff away from patients in the building. Needs from you: practice-approved information and fallback rules. Typical time: 3–5 business days.
- **Call-Overflow Handler** — Picks up when every line is busy, handles approved requests, and puts anything urgent straight through to a person. Result: A busy morning stops meaning unanswered phones and abandoned callers. Needs from you: call routing, scripts, and live escalation path. Typical time: 2–3 weeks. Uses AI calling; calling and AI usage is billed separately.
- **After-Hours Message & Escalation** — Takes overnight messages under your policy and directs genuine emergencies to your named escalation path immediately. Result: Owners get a clear instruction at 2am instead of a voicemail beep. Needs from you: after-hours policy and escalation contacts. Typical time: 2–3 weeks. Uses AI calling; calling and AI usage is billed separately.
- **Refill Request Router** — Collects refill requests on approved fields and routes them to the technician or veterinarian who authorises them. Result: Refill requests reach the right person with patient details attached, not as callback notes. Needs from you: approved request fields and staff ownership. Typical time: 5–8 business days.
- **Multi-Doctor & Location Router** — Routes requests by doctor, service type, location and documented ownership rules. Result: Requests reach the doctor or site that can actually see the patient. Needs from you: documented routing and ownership rules. Typical time: 3–5 weeks.

### Calls & Intake
- **New-Client Intake Workflow** — Gathers owner and patient details and requests prior records before the first visit. Result: New clients arrive registered, with history already requested. Needs from you: approved fields, document flow, and ownership. Typical time: 5–8 business days.
- **Records Request Workflow** — Captures records requests, confirms the approved recipient, and hands the release step to staff. Result: Records requests follow one documented process instead of ad-hoc emails. Needs from you: records-release process and approved recipients. Typical time: 5–8 business days.

### Scheduling
- **Reminder & Reschedule Workflow** — Confirms upcoming appointments and offers a new time when the owner cannot make it. Result: Fewer slots are lost to owners who simply forgot. Needs from you: schedule trigger and consent-aware messaging. Typical time: 5–8 business days.
- **Missed-Appointment Recovery** — Follows up after a missed visit with your approved wording and offers the next available time. Result: Missed appointments get one clear route back onto the schedule. Needs from you: appointment outcome and approved messaging. Typical time: 3–5 business days.
- **Waitlist & Opening Filler** — Offers cancelled slots to an eligible waitlist and stops the moment the opening is taken. Result: Same-day cancellations get refilled without anyone working down a list. Needs from you: eligible waitlist and schedule access. Typical time: 1–2 weeks.
- **Preventive-Care Reminder Sequence** — Sends approved reminders when vaccines, wellness visits or parasite prevention come due. Result: Preventive care gets booked on schedule rather than when the owner remembers. Needs from you: approved due list and messaging rules. Typical time: 1–2 weeks.

### Follow-Up
- **Inactive Client Reactivation** — Segments clients who have not visited in a defined period and runs a measured, consent-aware outreach. Result: Lapsed clients are invited back before they register somewhere else. Needs from you: approved audience and consent review. Typical time: 1–2 weeks.

### Operations
- **PIMS Administrative Sync** — Keeps approved client, patient and appointment records aligned between your PIMS and the tools around it. Result: Reception stops re-typing the same client into two systems. Needs from you: supported PIMS access and security review. Typical time: 3–5 weeks.
- **Client Access & Call Outcome Dashboard** — Shows call volume, abandoned calls, request types, response times and what was left unresolved. Result: Managers can see how much demand the phones are actually losing. Needs from you: calling and request-data access. Typical time: 3–5 weeks.

## What we do not do (boundaries)
Automation handles approved appointment requests, administrative questions, refill and records routing, and reminders. It does not triage, assess symptoms, judge urgency, or give any advice about an animal’s health.
- No triage and no clinical advice: The workflow never assesses symptoms, judges urgency on its own, or advises on an animal’s condition. It captures the request and moves it to a person.
- Emergencies reach a human immediately: Wording you define as an emergency triggers your escalation path — a live transfer or on-call contact — rather than a queue or a callback promise.
- Your approved answers only: The assistant works from a fixed set of administrative answers your practice has signed off, and hands over anything outside them.
- Staff keep the schedule: Bookings happen only inside approved appointment types, doctors and time blocks. Anything else becomes a task for reception.
- Usage costs stay separate: Calling, messaging, AI and software-provider usage is quoted apart from the monthly plan price.
- Launch includes failure-path testing: Duplicate requests, opt-outs, failed transfers, overnight calls and escalation contacts are tested before a client touches it.
