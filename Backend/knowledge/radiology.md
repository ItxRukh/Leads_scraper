## What we build
### Referrer Access
- **Referrer Inquiry Response Workflow** — Acknowledges referring-office inquiries in approved language, classifies the request type, and hands it to the named owner. Result: Referring offices get an answer the same day instead of waiting on a shared inbox. Needs from you: approved inquiry types, response copy, and owners. Typical time: 3–5 business days.
- **After-Hours Referrer Call Assistant** — Takes overnight and weekend referrer calls under your policy and directs anything urgent to the on-call path. Result: Overnight referrer traffic is captured without waking a radiologist for administrative questions. Needs from you: approved call flow, FAQs, and escalation rules. Typical time: 1–2 weeks. Uses AI calling; calling and AI usage is billed separately.
- **Referral Intake Completeness Workflow** — Checks incoming referrals against your required-field checklist and requests whatever is missing before it reaches the worklist. Result: Incomplete referrals get fixed up front instead of stalling the read. Needs from you: approved intake checklist and supported source access. Typical time: 5–8 business days.
- **Referral Status Update Workflow** — Sends approved status events back to referring offices at the points you define. Result: Referrers stop calling to ask where a study is in the process. Needs from you: approved status events, recipients, and message copy. Typical time: 1–2 weeks.
- **Missing Referral Information Follow-Up** — Chases the specific administrative items a referral is missing, using your checklist and wording. Result: Missing paperwork is resolved by a defined process rather than an assistant remembering. Needs from you: administrative checklist and messaging rules. Typical time: 3–5 business days.
- **Referrer FAQ Assistant** — Answers approved operational questions from referring offices — turnaround expectations, submission routes, contacts — and escalates the rest. Result: Routine referrer questions stop landing on partners and coordinators. Needs from you: approved referrer information and escalation path. Typical time: 5–8 business days.
- **Multi-Facility Contact Routing** — Applies facility, contract and ownership rules so every inbound contact reaches the right coordinator. Result: Requests stop bouncing between facilities before finding an owner. Needs from you: facility, contract, and ownership rules. Typical time: 1–2 weeks.
- **Referrer Service Issue Escalation** — Classifies service complaints by type and severity, applies your response targets, and escalates to the named owner. Result: Service problems reach leadership while they can still be fixed. Needs from you: issue types, response targets, and escalation owners. Typical time: 5–8 business days.
- **Referrer Follow-Up Queue** — Holds every open referrer request in one queue with an owner and a due time. Result: Nothing sits open because it was never anyone in particular’s job. Needs from you: request sources and owner rules. Typical time: 5–8 business days.
- **Multi-System Referrer Orchestration** — Coordinates approved referrer, coverage and request records across RIS, CRM and service-desk tools. Result: Referrer relationships stop being tracked in four disconnected places. Needs from you: system, security, and contract review. Typical time: 2–4 weeks.

### Calls & Intake
- **Coverage Inquiry Router** — Sorts coverage and scheduling requests by facility, subspecialty and contract, then routes to the person who owns that arrangement. Result: Coverage questions reach the right partner without three forwarded emails. Needs from you: coverage request fields and ownership map. Typical time: 1–2 weeks.
- **New Contract Inquiry Capture** — Captures new-business and contract inquiries on qualification fields and routes them straight to leadership. Result: Growth conversations do not sit in a general inbox for a week. Needs from you: qualification fields and leadership routing. Typical time: 3–5 business days.

### Coverage & Routing
- **Credentialing Request Coordination** — Tracks credentialing and privileging requests through your approved steps and prompts the owner at each stage. Result: Credentialing stops being the bottleneck nobody is watching. Needs from you: approved request types and secure system process. Typical time: 5–8 business days.

### Follow-Up
- **Response-Time Exception Alerts** — Alerts when a request passes the response target your group has committed to. Result: Service-level slips are caught in hours rather than at contract review. Needs from you: response targets and escalation paths. Typical time: 5–8 business days.

### Operations
- **Service Request Outcome Dashboard** — Shows request volume, type, response time and outcome by facility and referring group. Result: Partners can see referrer service performance with real numbers. Needs from you: administrative request and communication data access. Typical time: 2–4 weeks.
- **Referrer Access Leak Review** — A scoped review of where referrer requests are currently going unanswered, delivered as a written findings document. Result: You get a measured picture of the service gap before committing to a build. Needs from you: access to process and administrative outcome data. Typical time: 3–5 business days.
