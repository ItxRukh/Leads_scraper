## What we build
### Lead Capture
- **New-Matter Missed-Call Response** — Texts back callers the firm could not reach, records the general nature of the matter and their preferred contact time, then alerts the intake owner. Result: Missed callers reach intake with a documented request instead of an unreturned voicemail. Needs from you: business calling and approved response language. Typical time: 3–5 business days.
- **Instant Web-Inquiry Response** — Acknowledges website and campaign inquiries within seconds using approved language, and gathers what the form left blank. Result: Prospective clients get an immediate acknowledgment rather than silence overnight. Needs from you: lead source and approved reply flow. Typical time: 3–5 business days.
- **Matter-Type & Office Routing** — Sorts inquiries by practice area, jurisdiction and office, then hands each to the attorney or intake staffer who owns it. Result: Inquiries land with the right practice group rather than being forwarded around the firm. Needs from you: approved routing rules and staff ownership. Typical time: 5–8 business days.
- **24/7 Legal Intake Assistant** — Answers calls at any hour, states your disclaimers, gathers approved intake details, and transfers anything requiring judgment. Result: After-hours callers are captured with disclaimers stated and nothing resembling advice given. Needs from you: approved script, disclaimers, and escalation path. Typical time: 2–3 weeks. Uses AI calling; calling and AI usage is billed separately.
- **After-Hours Inquiry Capture** — Takes evening and weekend inquiries under your after-hours policy and readies them for the morning intake queue. Result: Weekend inquiries are worked Monday morning instead of lost by then. Needs from you: after-hours policy and staff escalation rules. Typical time: 2–3 weeks. Uses AI calling; calling and AI usage is billed separately.
- **Referral Intake & Follow-Up** — Captures referrals from other firms and past clients, records the source, and closes the loop with the referrer. Result: Referral sources get acknowledged, so they keep referring. Needs from you: referral fields and communication rules. Typical time: 1–2 weeks.

### Consultations
- **Consultation Request & Scheduling** — Offers consultation times inside the attorney availability and matter rules you define, and escalates anything outside them. Result: Consultations get booked with the right attorney and the right length reserved. Needs from you: calendar access and consultation rules. Typical time: 5–8 business days.
- **Consultation Reminder & Reschedule** — Confirms upcoming consultations and offers a new slot when someone cannot attend. Result: Fewer billable consultation slots are lost to no-shows. Needs from you: calendar trigger and approved messaging. Typical time: 3–5 business days.
- **Prospective Client Follow-Up** — Runs an approved cadence for consultations that have not converted, stopping on reply and alerting the assigned attorney. Result: Signed engagements stop depending on whether anyone remembered to follow up. Needs from you: matter status, owner, and stop rules. Typical time: 1–2 weeks.

### Client Intake
- **Structured New-Matter Intake** — Collects the intake fields your firm approves — parties, dates, jurisdiction, deadlines — and delivers them to a secure destination. Result: Every new matter arrives with the same fields completed and conflicts-check details ready. Needs from you: approved fields, privacy review, and secure destination. Typical time: 5–8 business days.
- **Document Checklist Follow-Up** — Reminds a prospective client which documents are still outstanding and gives them a secure way to send them. Result: Files arrive without a paralegal sending the same email four times. Needs from you: approved checklist and secure upload path. Typical time: 5–8 business days.
- **Engagement-Step Reminders** — Prompts the client through the steps between consultation and signed engagement using your approved language. Result: Fewer matters stall between "interested" and "engaged". Needs from you: approved milestone and engagement language. Typical time: 1–2 weeks.
- **Multi-Office Intake Orchestration** — Coordinates intake, routing, conflicts steps and follow-up across offices and practice groups under one documented process. Result: Every office runs the same intake, with ownership clear at each handoff. Needs from you: office rules, ownership, privacy, and security review. Typical time: 3–5 weeks.

### Case Follow-Up
- **Client Status-Request Routing** — Acknowledges "any update on my case?" messages with approved wording and routes them to the attorney or paralegal who owns the matter. Result: Status chasing stops interrupting attorneys and still gets answered. Needs from you: matter ownership and approved acknowledgment. Typical time: 5–8 business days.

### Operations
- **Practice-Management & CRM Orchestration** — Keeps approved lead, matter and follow-up records aligned across the systems the firm already runs. Result: Intake stops living in three places that disagree with each other. Needs from you: supported system access and data mapping. Typical time: 3–5 weeks.
- **Intake & Consultation Outcome Dashboard** — Shows inquiry source, response time, consultations booked, engagements signed, and where matters were lost. Result: Partners can see intake performance by practice area instead of guessing. Needs from you: lead, call, calendar, and outcome data access. Typical time: 3–5 weeks.

## What we do not do (boundaries)
Automation handles approved intake, scheduling, routing and follow-up. It does not give legal advice, assess the merits of a matter, quote fees, run conflicts checks, or create an attorney-client relationship.
