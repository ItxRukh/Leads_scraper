## What we build
### Quotes & Routes
- **Quote-Request Rescue** — Replies to quote requests within seconds, confirms the property address and services wanted, and offers estimate times. Result: Quote requests get answered the same hour instead of piling up through spring. Needs from you: lead source and approved messaging. Typical time: 2–3 business days.
- **Estimate Booking Workflow** — Books estimate visits inside your calendar, territory and route rules and escalates anything outside them. Result: Estimates get grouped into routes rather than scattered across the county. Needs from you: calendar and territory rules. Typical time: 3–5 business days.
- **Route-Density & Crew-Capacity Router** — Routes new work by neighbourhood density, crew skill, hours and available capacity. Result: New clients are added where a crew already drives, not across town. Needs from you: route and capacity data. Typical time: 1–3 weeks.
- **Recurring Service Follow-Up** — Follows up with one-off clients who never moved onto a recurring maintenance plan. Result: One-time cleanups convert into recurring route revenue. Needs from you: approved plan details. Typical time: 3–5 business days.
- **Crew Schedule-Gap Campaign** — When a crew day has holes, it offers those slots to clients with approved pending work. Result: Crew hours get used instead of sitting idle after a cancellation. Needs from you: schedule and customer data. Typical time: 5–8 business days.

### Lead Capture
- **Service-Area Checker** — Confirms the property falls inside your approved territory and route density rules. Result: Out-of-area quotes stop consuming estimator drive time. Needs from you: service territory rules. Typical time: 2–3 business days.
- **Smart Property & Service Intake** — Collects lot size, services wanted, access notes, gates, pets and frequency on your approved field list. Result: Estimators arrive with the property details they would otherwise gather by phone. Needs from you: approved intake fields. Typical time: 3–5 business days.
- **Missed-Call Text-Back** — Texts back anyone the office could not answer and captures what they need. Result: Missed callers get a reply instead of calling the next company on the list. Needs from you: business phone and SMS. Typical time: 2–3 business days.
- **Form-to-Field-Service Sync** — Writes approved lead and property details into your field-service system automatically. Result: The office stops re-typing the same property into a second system. Needs from you: supported system access. Typical time: 3–5 business days.

### Estimates
- **Open-Estimate Follow-Up** — Works open quotes on an approved cadence and alerts the owner the moment a client replies. Result: Quotes stay in play until the client answers or the job is closed out. Needs from you: estimate status data. Typical time: 5–8 business days.

### Scheduling
- **Estimate Reminder & Reschedule** — Confirms upcoming estimate visits and captures reschedules before a crew drives out. Result: Fewer estimator trips are wasted on clients who are not home. Needs from you: calendar and consent. Typical time: 3–5 business days.

### Follow-Up
- **Seasonal Client Reactivation** — Brings back last season’s clients before the new season starts, with approved wording and opt-out handling. Result: The route is rebuilt in February rather than scrambled for in April. Needs from you: client history and consent. Typical time: 5–8 business days.

Uses AI calling; calling and AI usage is billed separately.
- **Seasonal Reactivation Caller** — Calls last season’s clients with your approved script and passes live interest to the office. Result: Reactivation gets a call attempt, not just an email in a spring inbox. Needs from you: client data and call consent. Typical time: 5–10 business days. Uses AI calling; calling and AI usage is billed separately.

### Operations
- **Quote, Route & Capacity Dashboard** — Shows quote volume, response time, close rate, route density and crew capacity in one view. Result: You can see whether the bottleneck is leads, quotes or crew capacity. Needs from you: cRM, estimate, and route data. Typical time: 1–3 weeks.

Automation handles approved intake, estimate booking, reminders, follow-up and reactivation. It does not price work, measure properties, recommend plants or treatments, or promise a service date.

- Route rules come first: Territories, route density, crew skills, capacity and how far you will travel are documented before anything is built.
- Consent-aware seasonal outreach: Sending windows, frequency and opt-out handling are built in, which matters when the same list is contacted every season.
- Usage costs stay separate: Calling, messaging, AI and software-provider usage is quoted apart from the monthly plan price.
- Launch includes failure-path testing: Duplicate requests, opt-outs, failed transfers, seasonal volume spikes and staff notifications are tested before launch.
