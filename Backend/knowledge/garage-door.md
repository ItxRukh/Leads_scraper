## What we build
### Book Repairs
- **Urgent Repair Lead Rescue** — Texts back the caller within seconds, records the reported problem, address and how soon they need someone, then alerts the office. Result: A stuck-door call reaches the office with the details attached before the customer rings a competitor. Needs from you: business phone and approved SMS. Typical time: 2–3 business days.
- **Repair Appointment Booking** — Books inside your calendar, territory and job-type rules and hands anything outside them to staff. Result: Approved repairs become bookings rather than a callback promise. Needs from you: calendar and booking rules. Typical time: 3–5 business days.
- **After-Hours Request Capture** — Captures evening and weekend requests under your policy and readies them for the morning board. Result: A Saturday-night door failure is on Monday’s schedule instead of lost. Needs from you: after-hours policy. Typical time: 3–5 business days.

### Lead Capture
- **Service-Area Checker** — Confirms the address falls inside your approved territory before anything reaches dispatch. Result: Out-of-area calls are filtered early instead of consuming office time. Needs from you: service ZIP or map rules. Typical time: 2–3 business days.
- **Repair-Request Intake** — Collects door type, reported symptom, opener brand and access notes on your approved field list. Result: Technicians arrive knowing what they are walking into and what parts to carry. Needs from you: approved intake fields. Typical time: 3–5 business days.
- **Form-to-Dispatch Sync** — Writes approved request details straight into your dispatch or field-service system. Result: The office stops re-typing the same request into a second system. Needs from you: supported system access. Typical time: 3–5 business days.

### Repairs & Estimates
- **Price-Shopper Follow-Up** — Follows up with callers who only asked about price and never booked, using approved wording. Result: Price-shoppers get one clear route back instead of being written off at hello. Needs from you: approved messaging. Typical time: 3–5 business days.
- **Installation Estimate Follow-Up** — Works open door-replacement estimates on an approved cadence and alerts the owner on any reply. Result: Replacement quotes keep moving until the customer answers or the job closes. Needs from you: estimate status data. Typical time: 5–8 business days.

### Scheduling & Dispatch
- **Technician, Area & Job Router** — Routes each job by technician skill, territory, hours and available capacity. Result: Jobs reach a tech who can actually take them, close enough to make the window. Needs from you: technician and territory rules. Typical time: 1–3 weeks.
- **Repair Reminder & Reschedule** — Confirms upcoming visits and captures reschedules before a truck is dispatched. Result: Fewer wasted drive-outs to customers who are not home. Needs from you: calendar and consent. Typical time: 2–3 business days.

### Follow-Up
- **Past Inquiry Reactivation** — Re-engages people who enquired but never booked, with approved wording and opt-out handling. Result: Old inquiries get one considered follow-up rather than nothing. Needs from you: lead history and consent. Typical time: 5–8 business days.

Uses AI calling; calling and AI usage is billed separately.

## What we do not do (boundaries)
Automation handles approved intake, booking, routing and follow-up. It does not diagnose garage-door faults, give instructions for springs or cables under tension, promise an arrival time, or quote variable repair work.
- No diagnosis over the phone: The workflow records the reported symptom. Diagnosing the spring, opener or track stays with your technician on site.
- Safety topics escalate: Broken springs, cables under tension, a door off its tracks or anything you flag as unsafe transfers to a person immediately.
- No firm quotes on variable work: Flat-rate items can be stated if you approve them. Repairs and replacements route to a person.
- Dispatch keeps the board: Bookings happen only inside approved ZIPs, hours, job types and capacity. Everything else becomes a staff task.
- Usage costs stay separate: Calling, messaging, AI and software-provider usage is quoted apart from the monthly plan price.
- Launch includes failure-path testing: Duplicate requests, opt-outs, failed transfers, after-hours calls and staff notifications are tested before launch.
