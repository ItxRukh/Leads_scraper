## What we build
### Patient Access
- **Medical vs. Cosmetic Inquiry Router** — Asks the few questions needed to tell a medical concern from a cosmetic interest, then sends each down its own approved path. Result: Two very different demand types stop competing for the same phone queue. Needs from you: approved inquiry types and routing ownership. Typical time: 3–5 business days.
- **Missed-Call Recovery** — Texts back callers the desk could not reach and captures what they need in administrative terms. Result: Missed callers reach the queue with a documented request instead of a lost voicemail. Needs from you: business calling and approved messaging. Typical time: 3–5 business days.
- **Referral Intake Checklist** — Collects referral details and required documents on an approved checklist and routes them for staff review. Result: Referrals arrive complete rather than needing three calls back to the referring office. Needs from you: approved referral fields and review process. Typical time: 5–8 business days.
- **Appointment Request Capture** — Gathers visit-type and timing preferences on your rules and either books or hands a clean request to scheduling. Result: Requests arrive ready to schedule instead of as a callback note. Needs from you: visit-type and scheduling rules. Typical time: 5–8 business days.
- **Provider & Visit-Type Router** — Matches each request to the right provider, visit length and location using your documented rules and exceptions. Result: Patients land on a provider who treats their concern, at the right appointment length. Needs from you: documented schedule and exception rules. Typical time: 5–8 business days.
- **Approved Practice FAQ Assistant** — Answers the administrative questions you approve and escalates anything clinical or unclear. Result: Routine questions stop taking staff away from patient access work. Needs from you: practice-approved content and escalation rules. Typical time: 5–8 business days.
- **Dermatology AI Receptionist** — Answers approved calls, separates medical from cosmetic, books within your rules, and transfers anything clinical. Result: Calls are answered at volume, and clinical concerns still reach a person quickly. Needs from you: call flow, approved content, and live escalation. Typical time: 2–3 weeks. Uses AI calling; calling and AI usage is billed separately.
- **After-Hours Inquiry Capture** — Takes evening and weekend inquiries under your after-hours policy and readies them for the morning. Result: Evening cosmetic research and overnight concerns are both captured. Needs from you: after-hours policy and escalation contacts. Typical time: 2–3 weeks. Uses AI calling; calling and AI usage is billed separately.

### Medical & Cosmetic
- **Instant Cosmetic Lead Response** — Replies to cosmetic inquiries within seconds using approved service language and moves them toward a consultation. Result: Cosmetic leads get a reply while interest is high, without medical intake language. Needs from you: lead source, approved services, and messaging rules. Typical time: 3–5 business days.
- **Cosmetic Consultation Follow-Up** — Follows up on cosmetic consultations that did not convert, using only claims your team has approved. Result: Cosmetic interest is worked properly instead of fading after one visit. Needs from you: approved claims, cadence, and opt-out handling. Typical time: 1–2 weeks.
- **Approved Recall Reminder Sequence** — Sends reminders when skin checks or follow-up visits come due on your approved list. Result: Recall visits get booked on schedule rather than when a patient remembers. Needs from you: approved due list and outreach rules. Typical time: 1–2 weeks.
- **Medical, Cosmetic & Referral Dashboard** — Shows demand, response time and outcomes split by medical, cosmetic and referral source. Result: Leadership can see which side of the practice is losing demand and where. Needs from you: lead, call, referral, and outcome data access. Typical time: 3–5 weeks.

### Scheduling
- **Waitlist & Cancellation Filler** — Offers cancelled appointments to an eligible waitlist and stops as soon as the slot is claimed. Result: Cancellations get refilled without staff working down a call list. Needs from you: schedule access and eligible waitlist. Typical time: 1–2 weeks.
- **Pre-Visit Instructions & Reminders** — Sends approved preparation instructions and reminders ahead of the appointment. Result: Patients arrive prepared, so fewer visits need to be rescheduled on the day. Needs from you: approved content, schedule trigger, and consent rules. Typical time: 5–8 business days.
- **No-Show & Reschedule Recovery** — Reaches out after a missed appointment and offers the next available time. Result: No-shows get a route back onto the schedule instead of being written off. Needs from you: appointment outcome and approved messaging. Typical time: 1–2 weeks.

### Operations
- **EHR & Cosmetic CRM Access Sync** — Keeps approved patient, appointment and lead records aligned across the clinical and cosmetic systems. Result: The medical and cosmetic sides stop maintaining two disconnected pictures of the same patient. Needs from you: supported system access and security review. Typical time: 3–5 weeks.

## What we do not do (boundaries)
Automation handles approved routing, scheduling, reminders and follow-up. It does not evaluate skin concerns, assess urgency, discuss treatment or results, or replace a consultation with a provider.
- Clinical decisions stay with providers: The workflow separates and schedules demand. It does not assess a lesion, judge urgency, or advise on treatment.
- Medical and cosmetic are kept apart: Language, claims and routing differ by path, so a cosmetic lead never receives medical intake wording — or the reverse.
- Urgent concerns escalate: Wording you define as urgent transfers to staff immediately rather than being scheduled by automation.
- Privacy-aware by design: What is collected, where it is stored and who can see it is agreed before the build, and kept within the boundaries your practice already works to.
- Usage costs stay separate: Calling, messaging, AI and software-provider usage is quoted apart from the monthly plan price.
- Launch includes failure-path testing: Duplicate inquiries, opt-outs, failed transfers, after-hours calls and staff notifications are tested before a patient touches it.
