## Who this is for
We work with independent chiropractic clinics and growing multi-location practices. We usually speak with: Clinic owner, practice manager, or operations lead.

## The problems we fix (what owners tell us)
- New-patient calls go unanswered during adjustments
- Web inquiries wait too long for a reply
- First evaluations are missed and never rebooked
- Gaps in the schedule go unfilled
- Patients drift off their care plan unnoticed
- Inactive patients are never contacted
- Intake paperwork slows the first visit
- We cannot see which marketing produced new patients
- Each location runs a different process

## What we build
### New Patient Access
- **New-Patient Lead Rescue** — Replies to web and ad inquiries in seconds, asks what brought them in, and offers the next available evaluation. Result: New-patient inquiries get answered the same hour rather than the next morning. Needs from you: lead source and approved response. Typical time: 2–4 business days.
- **Missed-Call Text-Back** — Sends an immediate text to anyone the front desk could not reach and captures what they need. Result: Callers who would have tried the next clinic get a reply within a minute. Needs from you: business phone and approved SMS workflow. Typical time: 2–4 business days.
- **New-Patient Booking Workflow** — Books new patients into the visit type, location and time block your rules allow, and escalates anything else. Result: New patients get the right appointment length without the desk rebuilding it. Needs from you: visit, location, and scheduling rules. Typical time: 4–7 business days.
- **New-Patient Intake Checklist** — Guides a new patient through the forms you require before the first visit and flags what is still missing. Result: Patients walk in ready, so the first appointment is care rather than paperwork. Needs from you: approved form checklist and secure workflow. Typical time: 4–7 business days.
- **Approved Patient FAQ Assistant** — Answers the administrative questions you approve — hours, location, parking, insurance basics, what to bring. Result: Routine questions stop interrupting the desk during patient care. Needs from you: approved administrative content. Typical time: 4–7 business days.
- **Chiropractic AI Receptionist** — Answers approved calls, handles administrative questions, books within your rules, and transfers anything clinical. Result: The phone is answered during adjustments, and clinical questions still reach a person. Needs from you: approved call script, phone flow, and escalation rules. Typical time: 1–2 weeks. Uses AI calling; calling and AI usage is billed separately.
- **After-Hours Inquiry Capture** — Takes evening and weekend inquiries under your after-hours policy and readies them for the morning. Result: Pain does not keep office hours, and neither does your intake. Needs from you: after-hours policy and human escalation rules. Typical time: 1–2 weeks. Uses AI calling; calling and AI usage is billed separately.

### Scheduling
- **Appointment Reminder & Reschedule** — Confirms upcoming visits and offers a new slot when a patient cannot make it. Result: Fewer visits are lost to people who simply forgot the appointment. Needs from you: scheduling trigger and messaging consent. Typical time: 2–4 business days.
- **Missed-Evaluation Recovery** — Follows up when a first evaluation is missed and offers a clear path back onto the schedule. Result: A missed first visit gets one real attempt at rebooking instead of none. Needs from you: appointment status trigger and stop rules. Typical time: 4–7 business days.
- **Schedule-Gap Filler** — Offers openings to an eligible contact list when the day has holes, and stops as soon as they fill. Result: Gaps in the column get used instead of passing quietly. Needs from you: schedule access and eligible contact list. Typical time: 1–2 weeks.
- **Attendance Follow-Up Workflow** — Watches care-plan attendance and prompts the office when a patient starts drifting off schedule. Result: Drop-off is caught while the plan can still be recovered. Needs from you: appointment status and outreach rules. Typical time: 1–2 weeks.
- **Multi-Location Lead & Schedule Routing** — Routes inquiries to the correct clinic, provider and schedule rules. Result: Every location follows one intake process instead of its own habits. Needs from you: location, scheduling, and ownership rules. Typical time: 2–4 weeks.

### Follow-Up
- **Inactive-Patient Reactivation** — Segments patients who stopped coming and runs an approved, measured outreach with opt-out handling. Result: Lapsed patients get one considered invitation back rather than nothing. Needs from you: eligible patient list and approved outreach. Typical time: 1–2 weeks.
- **Review & Referral Request** — Asks for a review at the point you choose and routes any complaint privately to the office first. Result: Reviews come from satisfied patients; problems reach you before the internet. Needs from you: approved completion or satisfaction trigger. Typical time: 2–4 business days.

### Operations
- **Lead Source & Response Dashboard** — Shows where inquiries came from, how quickly they were answered, and which turned into a first visit. Result: Marketing spend can be judged on evaluations booked, not calls received. Needs from you: lead, call, and outcome data access. Typical time: 2–4 weeks.
- **Clinic System Orchestration** — Keeps approved patient, appointment and follow-up records aligned across the systems the clinic runs. Result: One update stops meaning three re-entries across separate systems. Needs from you: system, privacy, and security review. Typical time: 2–4 weeks.
