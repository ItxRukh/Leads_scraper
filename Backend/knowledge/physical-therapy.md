## Who this is for
We work with independent and multi-clinic physical therapy practices. We usually speak with: Owner, clinic administrator, or operations director.

## The problems we fix (what owners tell us)
- Referrals sit for days before anyone calls
- Missed calls are never returned
- Referring offices never hear that we made contact
- Patients drop out before the first evaluation
- Intake paperwork is incomplete on arrival
- Evaluation no-shows are not rebooked
- Open evaluation slots go unfilled
- Patients fall off their plan of care unnoticed
- We cannot see which referral sources convert

## What we build
### Referral Access
- **Referral-to-Evaluation Follow-Up** — Contacts a new referral the day it lands, explains the next step in approved wording, and keeps trying until the evaluation is booked or closed. Result: Referrals stop sitting in a queue waiting for someone to have a free hour. Needs from you: referral trigger, contact rules, and staff owner. Typical time: 3–5 business days.
- **Missed-Call Recovery** — Texts back patients and referrers who could not get through and captures what they needed. Result: Missed calls become named requests rather than an unknown number on the log. Needs from you: business calling and approved messaging. Typical time: 3–5 business days.
- **Referral Acknowledgement Workflow** — Confirms receipt back to the referring office so the physician knows the patient is being contacted. Result: Referring offices see that referrals are actioned, which is why they keep sending them. Needs from you: referral sources and acknowledgement rules. Typical time: 3–5 business days.
- **Evaluation Booking Request** — Offers evaluation times inside your therapist, clinic and visit-length rules, and escalates anything outside them. Result: Evaluations are booked with the right therapist and the right slot length. Needs from you: clinic and scheduling rules. Typical time: 5–8 business days.
- **PT AI Call Handler** — Answers approved calls, captures referral and scheduling details, and transfers anything clinical to a therapist. Result: The phone is answered while therapists are treating, without anyone giving clinical guidance. Needs from you: call flow, approved content, and live escalation. Typical time: 2–3 weeks. Uses AI calling; calling and AI usage is billed separately.
- **Referral Status & Handoff Routing** — Keeps each referral in a defined status with a named owner, and alerts when one stops moving. Result: No referral sits in limbo because nobody knew it was theirs. Needs from you: status definitions and ownership rules. Typical time: 1–2 weeks.
- **Referral Source & Multi-Clinic Dashboard** — Shows referral source, response time, evaluations completed and where patients dropped out, by clinic. Result: You can see which referring offices convert and which clinic loses patients before the first visit. Needs from you: referral, contact, and outcome data access. Typical time: 3–5 weeks.

### Evaluations
- **Evaluation Reminder & Reschedule** — Confirms upcoming evaluations and offers a new time when a patient needs to move. Result: Fewer first visits are lost to forgotten appointments. Needs from you: schedule trigger and messaging rules. Typical time: 5–8 business days.
- **Pre-First-Visit Follow-Up** — Sends approved arrival and preparation information ahead of the first evaluation. Result: Patients arrive prepared, so the first session is treatment rather than logistics. Needs from you: approved pre-visit content and schedule trigger. Typical time: 1–2 weeks.
- **Evaluation No-Show Recovery** — Follows up on missed evaluations with approved wording and a route back onto the schedule. Result: A missed first visit gets one real rebooking attempt instead of none. Needs from you: appointment outcome and stop rules. Typical time: 1–2 weeks.
- **Open Evaluation Slot Filler** — Offers open evaluation slots to an eligible waitlist and stops once they are taken. Result: Unused evaluation capacity gets filled instead of passing quietly. Needs from you: eligible waitlist and schedule access. Typical time: 1–2 weeks.

### Calls & Intake
- **Intake Completion Reminder** — Reminds patients about intake paperwork still outstanding before the first visit. Result: Evaluations start on time because the forms are already done. Needs from you: intake-status trigger and approved messages. Typical time: 5–8 business days.
- **Benefits & Document Checklist** — Collects insurance details, scripts and imaging on an approved checklist and routes them for staff review. Result: Authorisation problems surface before the visit, not at the front desk. Needs from you: approved checklist and staff review path. Typical time: 5–8 business days.

### Follow-Up
- **Attendance Exception Alerts** — Flags patients who are falling behind their plan of care using conditions your clinic defines. Result: Drop-off is caught while the plan can still be completed. Needs from you: practice-defined alert conditions. Typical time: 5–8 business days.
- **Past-Patient Reactivation** — Segments discharged patients who qualify and runs an approved, consent-aware check-in. Result: Previous patients return for new episodes rather than starting elsewhere. Needs from you: approved audience and consent review. Typical time: 1–2 weeks.

### Operations
- **EHR & CRM Administrative Sync** — Keeps approved patient, referral and appointment records aligned across the systems the clinic runs. Result: Front desk stops maintaining the same record in two systems. Needs from you: supported system access and security review. Typical time: 3–5 weeks.
