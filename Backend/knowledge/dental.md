## What we build
### New Patients
- **New-Patient Missed-Call Rescue** — Texts back the caller nobody could reach, asks what they need in plain administrative terms, notes when they are free, and hands the thread to the front desk. Result: A missed new-patient call becomes a staff task with the request and preferred times already written down. Needs from you: business phone and approved SMS workflow. Typical time: 2–4 business days.
- **Instant New-Patient Lead Response** — Replies to website, ad and contact-form inquiries in seconds, then guides the person toward booking or a staff callback. Result: Web inquiries get an immediate reply instead of waiting for someone to open the inbox. Needs from you: lead source and approved response. Typical time: 2–4 business days.
- **New-Patient Booking Workflow** — Offers only the visit types, providers and time blocks your rules allow, and turns anything outside them into a staff task. Result: New patients land on the right provider and visit length without the desk rebuilding the appointment. Needs from you: provider, visit-type, and scheduling rules. Typical time: 4–7 business days.
- **Digital New-Patient Intake Checklist** — Walks a new patient through the forms you require before the visit and tells the desk what is still outstanding. Result: Patients arrive with paperwork already done, so the chair is not used for clipboard time. Needs from you: approved checklist and secure form workflow. Typical time: 4–7 business days.
- **Benefits & Document Request Routing** — Collects insurance and document details on an approved list and routes each one to the person who handles it. Result: Verification requests reach the right staff member with the details attached, not as a sticky note. Needs from you: approved intake fields and staff ownership. Typical time: 4–7 business days.
- **Dental AI Receptionist** — Answers approved calls, handles administrative questions, books what your rules permit, and transfers anything clinical to staff. Result: Calls are answered even when the desk is with a patient, and clinical questions still reach a person. Needs from you: approved call script, phone flow, and escalation rules. Typical time: 1–2 weeks. Uses AI calling; calling and AI usage is billed separately.
- **After-Hours Inquiry Capture** — Takes evening and weekend inquiries under your after-hours policy and prepares them for the morning queue. Result: Nights and weekends stop being a hole in the new-patient pipeline. Needs from you: after-hours policy and staff escalation rules. Typical time: 1–2 weeks. Uses AI calling; calling and AI usage is billed separately.

### Scheduling
- **Appointment Confirmation & Reschedule** — Sends the reminder you approve, takes a confirmation, and offers a new time when the patient cannot make it. Result: Tomorrow’s column is confirmed the night before instead of guessed at in the morning. Needs from you: schedule trigger and messaging consent. Typical time: 2–4 business days.
- **Open-Chair Cancellation Filler** — When a slot opens, it offers the time to patients on your eligible waitlist and stops the moment someone accepts. Result: A same-day cancellation gets refilled without anyone phoning down a list. Needs from you: schedule access and eligible waitlist. Typical time: 1–2 weeks.
- **No-Show Recovery** — Reaches out after a missed appointment with your approved wording and offers the next available time. Result: No-shows get one clear route back to the schedule rather than being written off. Needs from you: appointment outcome and stop rules. Typical time: 4–7 business days.
- **Multi-Location Schedule & Lead Routing** — Routes each inquiry to the right office, provider and visit type using the rules each location actually runs on. Result: Every location follows one process instead of three different front-desk habits. Needs from you: location, provider, and scheduling rules. Typical time: 2–4 weeks.

### Case Follow-Up
- **Overdue Patient Reactivation** — Segments patients who are past due for hygiene or recall and runs a measured outreach that stops on reply or opt-out. Result: Overdue recall turns into booked hygiene instead of a report nobody works. Needs from you: eligible list and approved outreach. Typical time: 1–2 weeks.
- **Unscheduled Treatment Follow-Up** — Follows up on treatment the dentist recommended but the patient never scheduled, and alerts the assigned staff member on any reply. Result: Accepted-but-unscheduled treatment stays visible until it is booked or closed out. Needs from you: staff-approved recommendation status and outreach rules. Typical time: 1–2 weeks.
- **Review & Referral Request** — Asks for a review at the moment you choose, and sends anyone unhappy to a person before they reach a public page. Result: Satisfied patients get asked; concerns reach the office privately first. Needs from you: approved completion or satisfaction trigger. Typical time: 2–4 business days.

### Operations
- **Chair & Lead Outcome Dashboard** — Shows where inquiries came from, how fast they were answered, what got booked, and which chair time went unused. Result: An owner can see production lost to unanswered calls and empty chairs in one view. Needs from you: lead, call, schedule, and outcome data access. Typical time: 2–4 weeks.
- **Practice System Orchestration** — Keeps approved patient, appointment and follow-up records aligned across the systems the practice already runs. Result: One update stops meaning three re-entries across the PMS, CRM and spreadsheets. Needs from you: system, privacy, and security review. Typical time: 2–4 weeks.
