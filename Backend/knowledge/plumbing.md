## Who this is for
We work with residential and light-commercial plumbing service companies. We usually speak with: Owner, office manager, service manager, or dispatcher.

## The problems we fix (what owners tell us)
- Emergency calls go unanswered at night and on weekends
- Missed calls are never returned
- Web leads sit for hours before anyone replies
- Dispatch calls back just to collect basic details
- Urgent jobs are not flagged quickly enough
- Unsold estimates go quiet and stay quiet
- Past customers are never contacted again
- Call spikes bury requests nobody follows up
- We cannot see which marketing produced which job

## What we build
### Book More Jobs
- **After-Hours Missed-Call Rescue** — Texts back every unanswered caller, records what went wrong, where, and how soon they need someone, then pings the owner on duty. Result: Night and weekend callers land in the office queue with problem, address, timing and number already recorded. Needs from you: business calling and approved messaging. Typical time: 3–5 business days.
- **Instant Web-Lead Response** — Replies to form and ad leads within seconds, asks for whatever the form left out, and hands a booking-ready thread to the right person. Result: Online enquiries stop sitting unread — each one reaches an owner with the service details filled in. Needs from you: lead source and approved reply flow. Typical time: 3–5 business days.
- **Booking & Dispatch Handoff** — Books the slot your rules allow, or writes a dispatch task carrying the scope, address, stated urgency and the named owner. Result: Approved work becomes a booking; everything else becomes a task nobody has to reconstruct. Needs from you: schedule access and documented booking rules. Typical time: 5–8 business days.
- **24/7 Plumbing AI Receptionist** — Picks up approved calls at any hour, collects dispatch-ready details, books what your rules permit, and transfers anything unclear. Result: Calls are answered around the clock, approved jobs get booked, and grey areas reach staff with the context attached. Needs from you: call flow, booking rules, and live transfer path. Typical time: 2–3 weeks. Uses AI calling; calling and AI usage is billed separately.
- **Open Estimate Follow-Up** — Works an approved cadence across unsold estimates and notifies the assigned person the moment a customer writes back. Result: Quotes keep moving until the customer answers or the job is marked closed. Needs from you: estimate status and owner data. Typical time: 1–2 weeks.
- **Unscheduled Quote Recovery** — Goes back to people who asked what a job would cost but never booked anything, and offers them a defined next step. Result: Price enquiries that went quiet get one more clear route back to the calendar. Needs from you: eligible lead stage and approved outreach rules. Typical time: 1–2 weeks.
- **Maintenance & Replacement Reminder** — Uses your approved customer and equipment records to send reminders when service is due and routes interest back to the office. Result: Water heaters and service plans due for attention turn into conversations instead of forgotten rows. Needs from you: approved audience and reminder rules. Typical time: 1–2 weeks.
- **Lapsed Customer Reactivation** — Picks out past customers who qualify and runs a measured win-back sequence that stops on reply or opt-out. Result: Old customers get one considered approach — and anyone who says no is removed from the list. Needs from you: approved audience and consent review. Typical time: 1–2 weeks.

### Lead Capture
- **Structured Service Request Intake** — Walks the customer through the problem, property type, ZIP code, access and parking notes, preferred timing and contact details. Result: Dispatch opens every request with the same fields filled — no call back just to ask where the shut-off is. Needs from you: approved intake fields and service rules. Typical time: 5–8 business days.
- **Urgent Request Escalation** — Watches for the wording you flag as an emergency, shows your approved safety notice, and pushes the call to a live person or on-call alert. Result: Burst pipes and sewage backups reach a human immediately instead of joining a callback list. Needs from you: urgency definitions, notices, and escalation contacts. Typical time: 5–8 business days.
- **Busy-Line Call-Overflow Handler** — Takes over intake when every line is tied up, and keeps each unfinished request sitting visibly in the office queue. Result: A morning rush no longer buries requests that nobody ever called back. Needs from you: overflow trigger and handoff ownership. Typical time: 2–3 weeks. Uses AI calling; calling and AI usage is billed separately.
- **Commercial Service Request Router** — Separates commercial callers from residential, records the account and site details, and routes them to the estimator who owns that account. Result: Property managers reach the right estimator with the site and account already identified. Needs from you: commercial intake and ownership rules. Typical time: 5–8 business days.

### Scheduling & Dispatch
- **ZIP, Capacity & Job-Type Router** — Sends each request to the right branch or crew based on ZIP code, work type, operating hours, open capacity and your exceptions. Result: Jobs land with the team that can actually take them, not with whoever answers first. Needs from you: service-area, capacity, and ownership rules. Typical time: 1–2 weeks.
- **Field-Service & CRM Orchestration** — Keeps approved lead, customer, job, estimate and follow-up records in step across the systems your office already runs. Result: One update in the field is not three re-keyings back at the office. Needs from you: supported system access and data mapping. Typical time: 3–5 weeks.

### Estimates
- **Lead, Dispatch & Estimate Dashboard** — Puts source, stated problem, disposition, booking status, estimate stage, unresolved handoffs and branch patterns in one view. Result: An owner can trace a single call from where it came from to whether it ever earned revenue. Needs from you: call, lead, booking, and estimate data access. Typical time: 3–5 weeks.

### Operations
- **Job-Complete Customer Follow-Up** — Sends your approved close-out message, steers complaints privately to the responsible team, and asks for a review at the right moment. Result: Happy customers get asked for feedback; unhappy ones reach a person before they reach a review site. Needs from you: completed-job trigger and approved messaging. Typical time: 5–8 business days.

## What we do not do (boundaries)
Automation handles approved intake, booking, routing and follow-up. It does not diagnose plumbing faults, give instructions for gas or sewage situations, promise an arrival time, or quote variable repair work.
- Your dispatch rules come first: Service area, hours, job types, urgency wording, capacity limits, trip fees and transfer conditions get written down before a single workflow is built.
- No remote diagnosis, no firm quote: The workflow records what the customer says is wrong. Diagnosing the fault and pricing the repair stays with your technicians.
- Emergencies reach a person: Anything matching your emergency wording — gas, sewage, flooding, no water — transfers live or pages the on-call tech. It never waits in a queue.
- Dispatch keeps the board: Bookings happen only inside your approved ZIPs, hours, job types and capacity. Everything else becomes a task for a human to confirm.
- Usage costs stay separate: Calling, messaging, AI and software-provider usage is quoted apart from the monthly plan price, so you can see exactly what recurs.
- Launch includes failure-path testing: Duplicate submissions, opt-outs, failed transfers, outages, after-hours calls and staff notifications are all tested before a customer touches it.
