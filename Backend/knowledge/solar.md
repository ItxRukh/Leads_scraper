## What we build
### Lead Capture
- **Instant Solar Lead Response** — Responds to new web, referral, and partner leads within moments and moves each toward an approved consultation. Result: New solar leads get a prompt reply and enter a trackable consultation path. Needs from you: lead source and approved messaging. Typical time: 3–5 business days.
- **Missed-Call Lead Recovery** — Texts missed callers, captures their contact details, service address and request, then alerts the right sales owner. Result: Missed solar calls reach the correct sales owner with the details already captured. Needs from you: business phone and approved messaging. Typical time: 3–5 business days.
- **Service-Area & Project-Fit Checker** — Collects approved location, roof, utility, and timing details without making a technical or financial eligibility decision. Result: Each inquiry produces a consistent project-fit summary before it reaches sales. Needs from you: approved intake and service-area rules. Typical time: 3–5 business days.
- **No-Response Lead Nurture** — Runs an approved, stop-on-reply follow-up cadence when a prospect does not answer the first outreach. Result: Unresponsive prospects get a consistent cadence that stops when they reply, book, or opt out. Needs from you: pipeline stage and messaging consent. Typical time: 1–2 weeks.

### AI Calling
- **24/7 Solar AI Receptionist** — Answers approved calls, captures project details, offers consultation windows, and transfers technical conversations. Result: Approved calls are answered around the clock with clean handoffs to sales. Needs from you: call flow, booking rules, and live transfer path. Typical time: 2–3 weeks. Uses AI calling; calling and AI usage is billed separately.
- **After-Hours Lead Coverage** — Captures inquiries outside office hours and follows approved priority, transfer, and next-day handoff rules. Result: After-hours interest is captured instead of waiting for the next business day. Needs from you: after-hours policy and escalation contacts. Typical time: 2–3 weeks. Uses AI calling; calling and AI usage is billed separately.

### Consultations
- **Consultation Reminder & Reschedule** — Sends approved reminders ahead of a booked consultation and captures confirmation or reschedule requests. Result: Fewer no-shows on booked consultations and site visits. Needs from you: appointment trigger and messaging consent. Typical time: 3–5 business days.
- **Solar Consultation Booking** — Offers approved consultation windows to an eligible prospect, or creates a clean staff-confirmation request. Result: Eligible prospects reach an approved consultation window or an owned confirmation queue. Needs from you: calendar access and booking rules. Typical time: 5–8 business days.

### Site Surveys
- **Site-Survey Scheduling** — Moves qualified projects into approved survey windows and records access requirements and change requests. Result: Qualified projects move into a survey slot with access details already documented. Needs from you: survey calendar and eligibility trigger. Typical time: 5–8 business days.

- **Solar Proposal Follow-Up** — Keeps open proposals moving with approved reminders, question capture, and clear tasks for the advisor—no savings or approval claims. - **Installation & PTO Status Updates** — Sends approved milestone updates from permitting through installation, inspection, and utility permission-to-operate.
