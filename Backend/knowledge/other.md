Local service businesses with inbound calls and web enquiries and a small team. We usually speak with: Owner, general manager, service manager, operations lead, or dispatcher.

- Missed calls turn into lost jobs

- Web leads receive a slow response

- Past customers are not reactivated

## What we build
### Lead Capture
- **Missed-Call Job Rescue** — Texts back the caller nobody could reach, records what they need, where they are and when they are free, then alerts the office. Result: Missed callers become office alerts with the job details already written down. Needs from you: business calling and approved messaging. Typical time: 3–5 business days.
- **Instant Web-Lead Response** — Answers form and campaign leads within seconds, fills in whatever the form missed, and routes the reply to the right owner. Result: New web leads get a fast reply and reach the right person with context attached. Needs from you: lead source and approved reply flow. Typical time: 3–5 business days.
- **Service-Area Checker** — Confirms the address falls inside your approved territory before anything reaches the office. Result: Out-of-area requests are filtered early instead of eating staff review time. Needs from you: service ZIP or map rules. Typical time: 3–5 business days.
- **Structured Job-Request Intake** — Collects the reported problem, property details, timing, access notes and contact information without diagnosing anything. Result: Every request arrives with the details needed to qualify and route it. Needs from you: approved intake fields and service rules. Typical time: 5–8 business days.
- **24/7 Home Service AI Receptionist** — Answers approved administrative questions, captures job-ready details, and transfers anything technical, safety-related or uncertain. Result: More calls are answered with complete, staff-ready request details. Needs from you: approved call flow and live handoff rules. Typical time: 2–3 weeks. Uses AI calling; calling and AI usage is billed separately.
- **Form, Call & Field-Service Sync** — Creates or updates approved lead, customer and job records from calls, forms and messages in supported systems. Result: Customer and job records stay current without duplicate manual entry. Needs from you: supported system access and data mapping. Typical time: 5–8 business days.

### AI Calling
- **After-Hours Call Coverage** — Captures requests outside office hours under your priority, transfer and next-day handoff rules. Result: After-hours demand is captured instead of waiting for the office to reopen. Needs from you: after-hours policy and escalation contacts. Typical time: 2–3 weeks. Uses AI calling; calling and AI usage is billed separately.

### Scheduling & Dispatch
- **Booking & Dispatch Handoff** — Books inside your approved rules, or writes a staff-confirmation task carrying everything the next step needs. Result: Bookings reach dispatch with fewer missing details and less back-and-forth. Needs from you: schedule access and documented booking rules. Typical time: 5–8 business days.
- **Appointment Reminder & Reschedule** — Sends approved reminders and captures confirmations or reschedules before field time is wasted. Result: More appointments are confirmed or moved before the truck rolls. Needs from you: appointment trigger and messaging consent. Typical time: 3–5 business days.
- **Capacity & Territory Router** — Routes requests by geography, job type, hours, team capacity and your documented priorities. Result: Each request reaches the right territory and available team faster. Needs from you: service-area, capacity, and ownership rules. Typical time: 3–5 weeks.
- **Multi-Team Service Orchestration** — Coordinates intake, routing, booking, follow-up, record updates and reporting across branches or service teams. Result: Branches follow one connected process with fewer dropped handoffs. Needs from you: system, workflow, and security review. Typical time: 3–5 weeks.

### Estimates & Follow-Up
- **Open Estimate Follow-Up** — Runs a measured, stop-on-reply sequence across eligible open estimates and alerts the assigned team member. Result: Open estimates get consistent follow-up without daily staff chasing. Needs from you: estimate status and owner data. Typical time: 1–2 weeks.
- **Job-Complete Customer Follow-Up** — Sends an approved completion message, routes service concerns privately, and requests feedback at the moment you define. Result: Service issues surface privately while satisfied customers are asked for feedback. Needs from you: completed-job trigger and approved messaging. Typical time: 5–8 business days.

### Customer Retention
- **Lapsed Customer Reactivation** — Segments an approved past-customer audience and runs a relevant win-back with reply and opt-out handling. Result: Past customers re-enter the pipeline through timely, relevant outreach. Needs from you: approved audience and consent review. Typical time: 1–2 weeks.
- **Seasonal Customer Outreach** — Prepares approved reminders when the season, weather or service history makes the offer relevant. Result: Customers hear from you when seasonal demand actually applies to them. Needs from you: approved audience, trigger, and message. Typical time: 1–2 weeks.

### Operations
- **Lead & Service Operations Dashboard** — Shows source, response status, booking handoff, estimate stage and unresolved requests in one place. Result: Owners see response gaps, stalled estimates and unresolved requests without assembling a report. Needs from you: call, lead, booking, and estimate data access. Typical time: 3–5 weeks.
