## What we build
### Lead Capture
- **Missed Bid Inquiry Rescue** — Responds to missed calls from property owners, developers, and subcontractors, capturing project type, location, budget, and timeline before alerting estimating. Result: More bid opportunities are captured before a prospect calls another contractor. Needs from you: business phone number and qualification questions. Typical time: 3–5 business days.
- **Instant Bid Request Response** — Acknowledges website quote requests immediately, gathers missing project details, and routes each opportunity to the right estimator. Result: New project inquiries receive a professional response within minutes. Needs from you: lead form and approved reply flow. Typical time: 3–5 business days.
- **Project Location Qualifier** — Checks whether a project falls inside your approved service area or radius before it reaches estimating review. Result: Out-of-area opportunities are filtered automatically, saving review time. Needs from you: service radius or ZIP rules. Typical time: 3–5 business days.
- **Project Intake Assistant** — Collects project scope, drawings, budget, timeline, and customer information through a structured intake flow. Result: Every opportunity arrives at the estimator already organized and complete. Needs from you: intake questions and qualification rules. Typical time: 5–8 business days.

### AI Calling
- **24/7 Construction AI Receptionist** — Answers common questions, qualifies leads, captures project details, and transfers technical or emergency calls. Result: More calls are answered with complete project details already captured. Needs from you: call flows and live transfer rules. Typical time: 2–3 weeks. Uses AI calling; calling and AI usage is billed separately.
- **After-Hours Emergency Call Routing** — Captures after-hours calls and routes safety-critical or emergency situations to the correct on-call contact immediately. Result: Emergency and after-hours calls reach the right person without delay. Needs from you: after-hours policy and escalation contacts. Typical time: 2–3 weeks. Uses AI calling; calling and AI usage is billed separately.

### Scheduling & Dispatch
- **Site Visit Reminder & Reschedule** — Sends approved reminders ahead of a booked site visit and captures confirmation or reschedule requests. Result: Fewer missed site visits and less back-and-forth confirming them. Needs from you: appointment trigger and messaging consent. Typical time: 3–5 business days.
- **Site Visit & Estimate Scheduler** — Books site inspections or creates a staff-confirmation task, attaching the complete project details already captured. Result: Less scheduling back-and-forth for site visits and estimate appointments. Needs from you: calendar access and scheduling rules. Typical time: 5–8 business days.

### Estimating & Bidding
- **AI Bid Proposal Assistant** — Drafts a professional bid proposal from captured project information and your approved templates, ready for estimator review. Result: Faster proposal turnaround without a blank page every time. Needs from you: proposal templates and approved pricing inputs. Typical time: 1–2 weeks.
- **Open Bid Follow-Up** — Runs an approved, stop-on-reply follow-up cadence on submitted bids that have not received a decision. Result: Open bids get consistent follow-up instead of going quiet. Needs from you: bid status and approved messaging. Typical time: 1–2 weeks.

### Project Delivery
- **Project Milestone & Punch-List Updates** — Sends approved milestone and punch-list updates to owners and stakeholders as a project moves through delivery. Result: Clients stay informed without the office fielding every status call. Needs from you: project-stage data and approved messaging. Typical time: 1–2 weeks.

### Operations
- **Subcontractor & Vendor Confirmation Sync** — Creates or updates approved project, subcontractor, and vendor records from calls, forms, and messages in supported systems. Result: Project records stay current without duplicate manual entry. Needs from you: supported system access and data mapping. Typical time: 5–8 business days.
- **Crew & Territory Capacity Router** — Routes inquiries and scheduling requests by geography, crew availability, project type, and your documented priorities. Result: Each request reaches the right territory and available crew faster. Needs from you: service-area, capacity, and ownership rules. Typical time: 3–5 weeks.
- **Bid-to-Build Operations Dashboard** — Shows source, response status, bid stage, site-visit status, and project-delivery stage in one place. Result: Owners see response gaps and stalled bids without chasing spreadsheets. Needs from you: lead, bid, and project data access. Typical time: 3–5 weeks.
