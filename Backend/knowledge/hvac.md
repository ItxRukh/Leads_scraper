## What we build
### Book More Jobs
- **Missed-Call Job Rescue** — Responds to missed callers, captures service need, ZIP code, timing and contact details, then alerts the office. Result: Missed HVAC calls become office alerts with the job details attached. Needs from you: business calling and approved messaging. Typical time: 3–5 business days.
- **Instant Web-Lead Response** — Acknowledges form and campaign leads, collects the missing job details, and routes booking-ready replies promptly. Result: Web leads get a quick reply and reach the office with details filled in. Needs from you: lead source and approved reply flow. Typical time: 3–5 business days.
- **Service & Estimate Request Intake** — Separates repair, maintenance and replacement requests and gives dispatch the approved information it needs. Result: Each demand type enters its own dispatch path with the right fields. Needs from you: job types, service area, and required fields. Typical time: 5–8 business days.
- **Approved Service Booking** — Books or requests time slots only for approved job types, ZIP codes, hours and capacity conditions. Result: Only requests meeting your rules proceed to a booking step. Needs from you: schedule access and documented booking rules. Typical time: 5–8 business days.
- **24/7 HVAC AI Receptionist** — Answers approved calls, captures job details, books within defined rules, and transfers technical conversations. Result: Approved calls are answered around the clock with clean handoffs. Needs from you: call flow, booking rules, and live transfer path. Typical time: 2–3 weeks. Uses AI calling; calling and AI usage is billed separately.

- **Replacement Estimate Follow-Up** — Runs an approved stop-on-reply sequence for open replacement estimates and alerts the assigned person. Result: Replacement opportunities get consistent follow-up, not silence. Needs from you: estimate status and owner data. Typical time: 1–2 weeks.
