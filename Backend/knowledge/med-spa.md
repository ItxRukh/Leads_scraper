## Who this is for
We work with independent med spas and growing multi-location aesthetic practices. We usually speak with: Med spa owner, clinic manager, or marketing and operations lead.

## The problems we fix (what owners tell us)
- Paid leads wait hours for a reply
- Missed calls are never returned
- Leads who do not reply are never followed up
- Consultations get booked but not attended
- Consultation slots sit empty
- Past clients are never brought back
- Every location handles leads differently
- We cannot tell which ads produce treatments
- Follow-up depends on one person remembering

## What we build
### Lead Capture
- **Instant Consultation Lead Rescue** — Answers form and ad inquiries within seconds in your brand voice, asks what treatment they are considering, and offers a consultation time. Result: Paid leads get a reply while interest is still high instead of the next business day. Needs from you: lead source and approved response. Typical time: 2–4 business days.
- **Missed-Call Lead Rescue** — Texts back anyone who called and did not reach you, captures what they are interested in, and routes the thread to the front desk. Result: Missed calls become named leads with a stated interest rather than an unknown number. Needs from you: business phone and approved SMS workflow. Typical time: 2–4 business days.
- **Treatment-Interest Lead Concierge** — Answers approved questions about treatments, pricing ranges and preparation, then moves the conversation toward a consultation. Result: Common questions get answered instantly, and only real consultation candidates reach your team. Needs from you: approved service content and escalation rules. Typical time: 4–7 business days.
- **Premium AI Lead Concierge** — Answers calls in your brand voice, handles approved questions, books consultations within your rules, and transfers anything clinical. Result: Callers get a polished answer at any hour, and clinical topics still reach a provider. Needs from you: approved call script, brand voice, and escalation rules. Typical time: 1–2 weeks. Uses AI calling; calling and AI usage is billed separately.
- **After-Hours Consultation Capture** — Captures evening and weekend inquiries under your after-hours policy and prepares them for the morning. Result: Evening interest, when most aesthetic research happens, stops going unanswered. Needs from you: after-hours policy and escalation rules. Typical time: 1–2 weeks. Uses AI calling; calling and AI usage is billed separately.
- **Multi-Location Lead Routing** — Sends each inquiry to the right location, provider and booking rule set. Result: A lead reaches the location that can treat them, not whichever inbox is open. Needs from you: location, provider, and booking rules. Typical time: 2–4 weeks.

### Consultations
- **Consultation Reminder & Reschedule** — Confirms upcoming consultations, takes a yes, and offers a new slot when someone needs to move. Result: Fewer consultation slots are lost to people who simply forgot. Needs from you: booking trigger and messaging consent. Typical time: 2–4 business days.
- **Consultation Booking Workflow** — Books consultations inside your provider, treatment and calendar rules, and creates a staff task for anything outside them. Result: Consultations land on the right provider with the right length booked. Needs from you: consultation and scheduling rules. Typical time: 4–7 business days.
- **Consultation No-Show Recovery** — Follows up after a missed consultation with your approved wording and offers the next available time. Result: No-shows are rebooked rather than quietly dropped from the pipeline. Needs from you: appointment outcome and rescheduling rules. Typical time: 4–7 business days.
- **Open-Consultation Filler** — When consultation slots sit empty, it offers them to an eligible audience and stops once the calendar fills. Result: Empty consultation hours get used instead of quietly passing. Needs from you: calendar access and eligible audience. Typical time: 1–2 weeks.

### Follow-Up
- **No-Response Nurture** — Runs a measured sequence for leads who never replied, honouring quiet hours and stopping the instant someone responds. Result: Quiet leads get a few well-timed touches instead of nothing or too much. Needs from you: approved cadence, quiet hours, and stop rules. Typical time: 4–7 business days.
- **Inactive Client Reactivation** — Segments clients who have not been in for a while and runs an approved win-back with full opt-out handling. Result: Past clients return through timely, relevant outreach instead of a mass blast. Needs from you: eligible list, approved offer, and outreach consent. Typical time: 1–2 weeks.
- **New-Treatment Launch Follow-Up** — Introduces a new service to the audience you approve, with the claims and language your team has signed off. Result: A new treatment reaches the right existing clients without a manual campaign build. Needs from you: approved audience, offer, and marketing language. Typical time: 1–2 weeks.
- **Review & Referral Request** — Requests a review at the moment you define, and routes anyone dissatisfied to a person before they post publicly. Result: Happy clients are asked at the right time; concerns reach you first. Needs from you: approved completion or satisfaction trigger. Typical time: 2–4 business days.

### Operations
- **Treatment, Source & Capacity Segmentation** — Groups leads by treatment interest, marketing source and available provider capacity so outreach matches what you can actually deliver. Result: Campaigns stop promoting treatments the calendar cannot absorb. Needs from you: cRM, source, and capacity data access. Typical time: 2–4 weeks.
- **Lead-to-Consultation Dashboard** — Traces each lead from source through response time, consultation booked, and treatment purchased. Result: You can finally see which channel produces treatments, not just clicks. Needs from you: lead, CRM, booking, and outcome data access. Typical time: 2–4 weeks.

## What we do not do (boundaries)
Automation handles approved lead response, booking and follow-up. It does not assess suitability for a treatment, promise results, discuss medical history, or replace a provider consultation.
- Your brand voice, not a generic bot: Tone, vocabulary, emoji use and the claims you are willing to make are agreed before anything is written.
- No medical advice or outcome promises: Automation answers approved administrative and service questions. Suitability, results and anything clinical goes to a provider.
- Consent and quiet hours are enforced: Sending windows, frequency caps and opt-out handling are built in rather than left to whoever is running the campaign.
- Providers keep the calendar: Bookings happen only inside approved treatments, providers and time blocks. Everything else becomes a staff task.
- Usage costs stay separate: Calling, messaging, AI and software-provider usage is quoted apart from the monthly plan price.
- Launch includes failure-path testing: Duplicate leads, opt-outs, failed transfers, after-hours inquiries and staff notifications are tested before launch.
