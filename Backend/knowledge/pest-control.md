## What we build
### Initial Service
- **Peak-Season Lead Rescue** — Answers new inquiries within seconds during a demand spike, captures the pest and property details, and offers first-service times. Result: Seasonal spikes convert into booked first service instead of overwhelming the phones. Needs from you: lead source and approved messaging. Typical time: 2–3 business days.
- **Initial-Service Booking** — Books first services inside your route, service-type and capacity rules and escalates anything outside them. Result: New customers are booked into routes that can actually absorb them. Needs from you: calendar and service rules. Typical time: 3–5 business days.
- **Missed-Call Text-Back** — Texts back anyone the office could not answer and captures what they need. Result: Missed callers get a reply within a minute instead of trying the next company. Needs from you: business phone and SMS. Typical time: 2–3 business days.

### Lead Capture
- **Approved Pest-Service FAQ** — Answers only the questions you approve — treatment timing, pet and child safety statements, preparation, what to expect — and hands over the rest. Result: Repetitive questions stop consuming customer-service capacity. Needs from you: approved FAQ content. Typical time: 2–3 business days.
- **Service-Area Checker** — Confirms the address falls inside your approved territory before anything reaches the office. Result: Out-of-area requests are filtered before they take up staff time. Needs from you: territory rules. Typical time: 2–3 business days.
- **Form-to-Field-Service Sync** — Writes approved lead and customer details into your field-service system automatically. Result: The office stops re-typing the same customer into a second system. Needs from you: supported system access. Typical time: 3–5 business days.

### Scheduling
- **Appointment Reminder & Reschedule** — Confirms upcoming services, sends preparation instructions, and captures reschedules. Result: Fewer stops are wasted on properties that were not prepared or nobody was home. Needs from you: calendar and consent. Typical time: 3–5 business days.
- **Route & Customer Exception Alerts** — Alerts the office when a stop is missed, a customer goes unserviced, or a route breaks your rules. Result: Service failures surface the same day rather than at the next billing cycle. Needs from you: workflow trigger data. Typical time: 3–5 business days.

### Renewals
- **Recurring-Plan Follow-Up** — Follows up with customers who took a one-off treatment but never moved onto a recurring plan. Result: One-time jobs convert into recurring revenue instead of ending after one visit. Needs from you: approved plan details. Typical time: 3–5 business days.

- **Territory, Season & Churn Segmentation** — Groups customers by territory, service history, season and churn risk so outreach matches route capacity. Result: Campaigns stop generating demand the routes cannot service. Needs from you: customer and territory data. Typical time: 1–3 weeks.

## What we do not do (boundaries)
Automation handles approved intake, booking, reminders, renewals and follow-up. It does not identify pests, recommend treatments, or give any advice about chemical safety, re-entry times, pets or health effects.
- Approved answers only: The assistant works from a fixed set of statements your team has signed off — treatment timing, preparation, safety wording — and hands over anything else.
- No chemical or safety advice: Automation never advises on product safety, re-entry times, pets, children or health effects. Those questions route to a qualified person.
- No pest identification: The workflow records what the customer says they saw. Identifying the pest and choosing treatment stays with your technicians.
- Routes keep the schedule: Bookings happen only inside approved territories, service types and route capacity. Everything else becomes a staff task.
- Usage costs stay separate: Calling, messaging, AI and software-provider usage is quoted apart from the monthly plan price.
- Launch includes failure-path testing: Duplicate requests, opt-outs, failed transfers, peak-volume spikes and staff notifications are tested before launch.
