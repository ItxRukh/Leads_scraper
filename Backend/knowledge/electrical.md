- Dispatch calls back just to collect basic details

- Open estimates go quiet

- Jobs reach techs without the right skill or licence

## What we build
### Service Calls
- **Missed-Call Service Capture** — Texts back callers the office could not reach, records the reported issue, address and timing, and alerts the team. Result: Missed service calls become office alerts with the job details attached. Needs from you: business phone and SMS. Typical time: 2–3 business days.
- **Call Summary & Team Alert** — Turns each captured call into a short written summary and posts it to the channel your team already watches. Result: The office sees what a caller wanted without replaying the recording. Needs from you: call source and alert channel. Typical time: 2–3 business days.
- **After-Hours Service Intake** — Captures evening and weekend requests under your policy and escalates anything matching your emergency wording. Result: Overnight demand is captured, and genuine emergencies reach the on-call tech. Needs from you: after-hours escalation policy. Typical time: 3–5 business days.

### Lead Capture
- **Job-Type & ZIP Intake** — Collects the job type, property details and ZIP code on your approved field list before anything reaches dispatch. Result: Dispatch gets a request it can act on rather than a name and a number. Needs from you: approved intake fields. Typical time: 3–5 business days.
- **Service-Area Checker** — Confirms the address is inside your approved territory before it reaches the office. Result: Out-of-area calls are filtered before they take up staff time. Needs from you: service ZIP or map rules. Typical time: 2–3 business days.
- **Form-to-Field-Service Sync** — Writes approved lead and job details into your field-service system automatically. Result: The office stops re-typing the same request into a second system. Needs from you: supported system access. Typical time: 3–5 business days.

### Scheduling & Dispatch
- **Booking Request & Dispatch Handoff** — Books inside your approved rules or creates a dispatch task carrying scope, address and stated urgency. Result: Approved work becomes a booking; everything else becomes a clean task. Needs from you: calendar and dispatch rules. Typical time: 3–5 business days.
- **Technician Schedule-Gap Campaign** — When the board has holes, it offers those slots to customers with approved pending work. Result: Empty technician hours get filled from work you already have. Needs from you: schedule and customer data. Typical time: 5–8 business days.
- **Skill, Geography & Capacity Router** — Routes each job by technician skill, licence level, location, hours and available capacity. Result: Jobs reach a tech who is qualified and close enough to take them. Needs from you: technician and service rules. Typical time: 1–3 weeks.
- **Service Appointment Reminders** — Confirms upcoming appointments and captures reschedules before a truck is dispatched. Result: Fewer wasted drive-outs to customers who are not home. Needs from you: appointment trigger and consent. Typical time: 2–3 business days.

### Estimates
- **Open-Estimate Follow-Up** — Works open estimates on an approved cadence and alerts the owner the moment the customer replies. Result: Panel upgrades and rewire quotes keep moving instead of going quiet. Needs from you: estimate status data. Typical time: 5–8 business days.

### AI Calling
- **Electrical AI Receptionist** — Answers approved calls, captures job details, and transfers anything technical or safety-related to a person. Result: Calls are answered while techs are in the field, with safety topics escalated immediately. Needs from you: approved script and handoff rules. Typical time: 5–10 business days. Uses AI calling; calling and AI usage is billed separately.
- **Estimate Follow-Up Caller** — Calls customers with open estimates using your approved script and passes interested conversations to staff. Result: Estimates get a call, not just an email nobody opened. Needs from you: estimate data and call consent. Typical time: 5–10 business days. Uses AI calling; calling and AI usage is billed separately.

### Operations
- **Daily Service Operations Digest** — Sends one daily summary of calls captured, jobs booked, estimates open and requests still unresolved. Result: Owners get a clear morning picture without opening four systems. Needs from you: workflow data access. Typical time: 3–5 business days.
- **Estimate & Service Pipeline Dashboard** — Shows source, response time, booking status, estimate stage and unresolved requests in one view. Result: You can see where service revenue is leaking rather than guessing. Needs from you: cRM and field-service data. Typical time: 1–3 weeks.
- **Multi-Branch Service Orchestration** — Coordinates intake, routing, booking and follow-up across branches under one documented process. Result: Branches run the same process with ownership clear at each handoff. Needs from you: system and security review. Typical time: 1–3 weeks.

## What we do not do (boundaries)
Automation handles approved intake, booking, routing and follow-up. It does not diagnose electrical faults, give troubleshooting or safety instructions, promise an arrival time, or quote variable work.
- Safety language escalates immediately: Burning smells, sparking, shocks, exposed wiring and anything you define as unsafe transfer to a person straight away.
- No remote diagnosis or troubleshooting: Automation never talks a customer through an electrical issue, and never suggests they check or touch anything themselves.
- No firm quotes on variable work: Flat-rate items can be stated if you approve them. Diagnostics, panel work and rewires route to a person.
- Dispatch keeps the board: Bookings happen only inside approved ZIPs, hours, job types and capacity. Everything else becomes a staff task.
- Usage costs stay separate: Calling, messaging, AI and software-provider usage is quoted apart from the monthly plan price.
- Launch includes failure-path testing: Duplicate requests, opt-outs, failed transfers, after-hours calls and escalation contacts are tested before launch.
