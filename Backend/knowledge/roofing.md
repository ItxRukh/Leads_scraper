# Roofing Companies: what we offer and how it helps

## Who this is for
We work with residential roofing companies with active lead generation and sales teams. We usually speak with: Owner, sales manager, or production manager.

## The problems we fix (what owners tell us)
- Leads wait hours before a rep calls
- Missed calls are never returned
- Leads sit unclaimed with no owner
- Leads who do not reply are never worked again
- Proposals go quiet and stay quiet
- Inspections are missed and not rebooked
- Storm weeks bury the lead queue
- The CRM pipeline does not reflect reality
- We cannot tell which lead source produces jobs

## What we build
### Book Inspections
- **Instant Inspection Lead Response** — Replies to a new lead within seconds, confirms the address and damage type in plain terms, and offers inspection times. Result: Leads get contacted while they are still comparing roofers, not the next afternoon. Needs from you: lead source and approved messaging. Typical time: 2–3 business days.
- **Inspection Booking Workflow** — Books inspections inside your calendar, territory and rep-availability rules and escalates anything outside them. Result: Inspections are booked with a rep who can actually cover that territory. Needs from you: calendar and territory rules. Typical time: 3–5 business days.
- **Storm-Surge Lead Router** — Handles volume spikes after a storm by routing on territory, capacity and priority rules you set in advance. Result: A storm week does not bury leads under a queue nobody can work. Needs from you: territory and capacity data. Typical time: 1–3 weeks.
- **Inspection Reminder & Reschedule** — Confirms upcoming inspections and offers a new time when the homeowner needs to move. Result: Fewer rep drive-outs are wasted on homeowners who are not home. Needs from you: calendar and approved reminders. Typical time: 2–3 business days.
- **Inspection No-Show Recovery** — Follows up when an inspection is missed and offers the next available slot. Result: Missed inspections get rebooked rather than written off as bad leads. Needs from you: appointment status trigger. Typical time: 3–5 business days.

### Lead Capture
- **Missed-Call Roofing Lead Recovery** — Texts back anyone the office could not answer and captures what they need. Result: Missed calls become named leads instead of unknown numbers on the log. Needs from you: business phone and SMS. Typical time: 2–3 business days.
- **Lead-to-Rep Routing** — Assigns each lead to a rep by territory, workload and your documented rules, and records the owner. Result: Leads stop sitting unclaimed because nobody knew whose they were. Needs from you: rep and territory rules. Typical time: 3–5 business days.
- **Lead Form-to-CRM Sync** — Writes approved lead details straight into the CRM with source and campaign attached. Result: Leads stop being re-typed, and attribution survives the handoff. Needs from you: cRM access. Typical time: 3–5 business days.

### Inspections & Estimates
- **Estimate & Proposal Follow-Up** — Follows open proposals on an approved cadence and alerts the assigned rep the moment the homeowner replies. Result: Proposals stay in play until the homeowner answers or the job is closed out. Needs from you: estimate status data. Typical time: 5–8 business days.

### Follow-Up
- **No-Response Lead Nurture** — Runs a measured sequence for leads who never replied, stopping on response or opt-out. Result: Quiet leads get several well-timed touches instead of one voicemail. Needs from you: cRM stage and messaging consent. Typical time: 5–8 business days.
- **Old Roofing Lead Reactivation** — Segments aged leads that qualify and runs an approved win-back with full opt-out handling. Result: Leads bought months ago get one more real attempt instead of sitting dead in the CRM. Needs from you: lead history and consent. Typical time: 5–8 business days.

### AI Calling
- **Roofing Lead Calling** — Calls new leads with your approved script, qualifies the basics, and hands live conversations to a rep. Result: Every lead gets a call attempt, not just the ones a rep got to. Needs from you: approved call script and handoff. Typical time: 5–10 business days. Uses AI calling; calling and AI usage is billed separately.

### Operations
- **Rep Follow-Up Task Automation** — Creates the follow-up tasks and reminders a rep should have set, based on lead stage and activity. Result: Follow-up stops depending on which rep is organised. Needs from you: cRM or notification channel. Typical time: 3–5 business days.
- **Lead Source & Disposition Dashboard** — Shows every lead by source, response time, rep, inspection booked and job sold. Result: You can tell which lead source actually produces roofs, not just phone calls. Needs from you: cRM, call, and source data. Typical time: 1–3 weeks.
- **Roofing Pipeline Hygiene Workflow** — Flags stale stages, missing dispositions and leads with no next step so the pipeline reflects reality. Result: Forecasts stop being built on a pipeline nobody has cleaned in months. Needs from you: cRM pipeline access. Typical time: 1–3 weeks.
- **Multi-Market Lead Orchestration** — Coordinates intake, routing, follow-up and reporting across markets under one documented process. Result: Every market runs the same lead process instead of its own version. Needs from you: system and security review. Typical time: 1–3 weeks.

## What we do not do (boundaries)
Automation handles approved lead response, booking, routing and follow-up. It does not assess roof damage, quote work, interpret insurance policies, or advise on claims.
- Your sales rules come first: Territories, rep assignment, response targets and what a qualified lead means are documented before anything is built.
- No pricing and no damage assessment: Automation captures what the homeowner reports. Assessing damage and pricing the roof stays with your reps.
- No insurance advice: The workflow does not interpret policies, estimate claim outcomes, or advise a homeowner on how to file.
- Consent is enforced: Sending windows, frequency limits and opt-out handling are built in rather than left to whoever runs the campaign.
- Usage costs stay separate: Calling, messaging, AI and software-provider usage is quoted apart from the monthly plan price.
- Launch includes failure-path testing: Duplicate leads, opt-outs, failed transfers, storm-volume spikes and rep notifications are tested before launch.
