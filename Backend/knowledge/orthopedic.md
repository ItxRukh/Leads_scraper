- Calls are abandoned before anyone answers
- Referrals sit for days before patient contact
- Referring offices never hear what happened
- Web inquiries wait too long for a reply
- Requests reach the wrong provider or service line

- Open provider slots are not refilled
- Intake and imaging arrive incomplete

## What we build
### Patient Access
- **Missed-Call Consultation Recovery** — Texts back callers the practice could not reach, records what they are asking about administratively, and puts the request in the access queue. Result: Missed callers reach staff with a documented request instead of disappearing from the log. Needs from you: business phone and approved SMS workflow. Typical time: 3–5 business days.
- **Orthopedic AI Receptionist** — Answers approved calls, handles the administrative questions you define, and transfers anything clinical or uncertain to staff. Result: Calls are answered at peak volume without anyone assessing an injury over the phone. Needs from you: approved call flow, FAQs, and escalation rules. Typical time: 1–2 weeks. Uses AI calling; calling and AI usage is billed separately.
- **Referral Response & Handoff** — Contacts the patient after a referral arrives and confirms back to the referring office that contact was made. Result: Referring physicians see their referrals actioned, which protects the relationship. Needs from you: referral sources, ownership, and approved response copy. Typical time: 1–2 weeks.
- **Web Inquiry Speed-to-Response** — Replies to website and campaign inquiries within seconds and collects what the form did not capture. Result: Web inquiries get an immediate acknowledgment rather than a next-day callback. Needs from you: lead source and approved messaging. Typical time: 3–5 business days.
- **Consultation Request Follow-Up** — Keeps working requests that have not yet become an appointment, with approved messaging and defined stop rules. Result: Requests stop stalling silently between the first call and a booked visit. Needs from you: request-status trigger and approved messaging. Typical time: 5–8 business days.
- **Approved Request Routing** — Applies your routing matrix — body part, provider, location, urgency category — and sends anything unusual to a named exception owner. Result: Requests reach the right provider without staff interpreting the matrix by memory. Needs from you: practice-approved routing matrix and exception path. Typical time: 1–2 weeks.
- **Pre-Visit FAQ Assistant** — Answers approved pre-visit questions — location, parking, imaging to bring, paperwork, arrival time — and hands over anything else. Result: Routine pre-visit questions stop consuming access-team capacity. Needs from you: approved patient information and human handoff. Typical time: 5–8 business days.
- **Incomplete Intake Follow-Up** — Reminds patients about the intake items still outstanding, on the timing and wording you approve. Result: Visits are not rescheduled at the desk because paperwork or imaging is missing. Needs from you: approved checklist, timing, and message copy. Typical time: 5–8 business days.
- **Patient Access Exception Alerts** — Alerts the access team when a request breaches the response time or ownership rules the practice has set. Result: Requests that stop moving surface the same day rather than at month end. Needs from you: practice-defined response targets and owners. Typical time: 5–8 business days.
- **Multi-System Access Orchestration** — Coordinates approved patient, referral and appointment records across EHR, scheduling and CRM systems. Result: Access work stops being re-keyed between three systems that disagree. Needs from you: system, security, and workflow review. Typical time: 2–4 weeks.

### Referrals
- **Referral Intake Capture** — Collects referral details on your approved field list and routes each one to the team that owns that body part or service line. Result: Referrals arrive complete and land with the right service line the first time. Needs from you: approved intake fields and routing rules. Typical time: 5–8 business days.
- **Referral Source Status Updates** — Sends approved status events back to referring offices so they know where their patient is in the process. Result: Referring offices stop calling to ask what happened to their patient. Needs from you: approved status events and recipient rules. Typical time: 1–2 weeks.

### Scheduling
- **Unconfirmed Consultation Recovery** — Chases appointments that were never confirmed and offers a new time when the patient cannot attend. Result: Unconfirmed slots are resolved before they become empty provider time. Needs from you: scheduling trigger and messaging consent. Typical time: 5–8 business days.
- **Open-Slot Waitlist Outreach** — Offers openings to patients who match your eligibility rules and stops once the slot is claimed. Result: Cancelled provider time gets refilled without a staff phone tree. Needs from you: calendar access and approved eligibility rules. Typical time: 1–2 weeks.

### Operations
- **Inquiry Outcome Dashboard** — Shows inquiry volume, response times, referrals converted to visits, and where requests were lost. Result: Administrators can measure access performance instead of estimating it. Needs from you: inquiry, scheduling, and communication data access. Typical time: 2–4 weeks.
- **Orthopedic Access Leak Review** — A scoped review of where inquiries, referrals and appointments are currently being lost, delivered as a written findings document. Result: You get a measured picture of the access leak before committing to any build. Needs from you: access to process and administrative outcome data. Typical time: 3–5 business days.
