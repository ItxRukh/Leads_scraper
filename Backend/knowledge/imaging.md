# Independent Imaging Centers: what we offer and how it helps

Source: our agency website. Use this to answer questions. If something is not written here, say you will confirm it on a short call. Never quote prices.

## Who this is for
We work with independent imaging centers with 2–20 locations. We usually speak with: Administrator, COO, or owner.

## The problems we fix (what owners tell us)
- Calls are abandoned before anyone answers
- After-hours requests are never captured
- Referrals arrive missing orders or insurance details
- Requests never reach a booked appointment
- Preparation questions consume the phone queue
- Unconfirmed appointments become no-shows
- Scanner time sits idle after cancellations
- Ordering offices call to chase status
- We cannot measure where requests are lost

## What we build
### Patient Access
- **Missed-Call Scan Request Recovery** — Texts back callers who could not get through, captures the exam requested and their availability, and puts it in the scheduling queue. Result: Abandoned calls become scheduled scans instead of business lost to the next center. Needs from you: business phone and approved SMS workflow. Typical time: 3–5 business days.
- **After-Hours Patient Access Assistant** — Answers evening and weekend calls under your policy, handles approved questions, and prepares requests for the morning. Result: Evening callers are captured rather than told to try again tomorrow. Needs from you: call flow, approved FAQs, and escalation rules. Typical time: 1–2 weeks. Uses AI calling; calling and AI usage is billed separately.
- **Appointment Request Follow-Up** — Follows up on requests that came in but never reached a booked appointment. Result: Requests stop stalling between "interested" and "scheduled". Needs from you: request source and approved messaging. Typical time: 3–5 business days.
- **Exam Preparation FAQ Assistant** — Answers the preparation questions you approve — fasting, contrast, arrival time, what to bring, implant paperwork — and escalates anything else. Result: Preparation questions stop occupying the phones and fewer patients arrive unprepared. Needs from you: approved patient information. Typical time: 5–8 business days.
- **Abandoned Request Recovery** — Re-engages people who started a request online or by phone and never finished it. Result: Half-finished requests get one clear route back rather than being deleted. Needs from you: request-status trigger and approved messaging. Typical time: 3–5 business days.
- **Multi-Location Request Routing** — Routes each request to the site with the right modality, availability and travel distance for the patient. Result: Patients reach the location that can actually perform the exam soonest. Needs from you: location and scheduling rules. Typical time: 1–2 weeks.
- **Patient Access Exception Alerts** — Alerts the team when a request breaches the response time or ownership rules you set. Result: Stalled requests surface the same day rather than at month end. Needs from you: defined response targets and owners. Typical time: 5–8 business days.

### Calls & Intake
- **Referral Intake Capture** — Collects order, insurance and patient details on your approved checklist and routes each one to the right team. Result: Referrals arrive complete instead of needing three calls back to the ordering office. Needs from you: approved intake fields and routing rules. Typical time: 5–8 business days.
- **Referrer Status Update Workflow** — Sends approved status events back to the ordering office so they know the exam is scheduled or complete. Result: Ordering offices stop calling to chase their own referrals. Needs from you: approved status events and recipient rules. Typical time: 1–2 weeks.
- **Referral Follow-Up Sequence** — Runs a measured sequence for referrals that have not yet been scheduled, with defined stop rules. Result: Unscheduled referrals get worked consistently rather than when someone has time. Needs from you: referral status and approved messaging. Typical time: 5–8 business days.

### Scheduling
- **Unconfirmed Appointment Recovery** — Chases appointments that were never confirmed and offers a new time where needed. Result: Unconfirmed slots are resolved before they become idle scanner time. Needs from you: scheduling trigger and messaging consent. Typical time: 5–8 business days.
- **Open-Slot Waitlist Workflow** — Offers open scanner time to an eligible waitlist and stops once the slot is taken. Result: Cancelled scanner time gets used rather than sitting idle. Needs from you: calendar access and eligible waitlist. Typical time: 1–2 weeks.

### Operations
- **Intake-to-System Sync** — Writes approved request details into your scheduling or RIS record instead of leaving them in a form. Result: Staff stop re-typing the same request into a second system. Needs from you: supported system access. Typical time: 5–8 business days.
- **Call Outcome Dashboard** — Shows call volume, abandonment, request types, response times and unresolved requests by location. Result: Leadership can see how much scan volume the phone queue is losing. Needs from you: call and request data access. Typical time: 2–4 weeks.
- **Multi-System Access Orchestration** — Coordinates approved request, patient and appointment records across scheduling, RIS and CRM. Result: One request stops living in three systems with three different statuses. Needs from you: system and security review. Typical time: 2–4 weeks.
- **Patient Access Leak Review** — A scoped review of where scan requests are currently being lost, delivered as a written findings document. Result: You see the measured leak before committing to any build. Needs from you: access to process and outcome data. Typical time: 3–5 business days.
